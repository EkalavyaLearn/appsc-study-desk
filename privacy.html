Website privacy policy

Effective 10 November 2026

APPSC Study Desk is a free, ad-free education website provided by EkalavyaLearn. It is independent and is not affiliated with APPSC or any government agency. This notice covers this browser edition.

Your study information

Lesson checklists, quiz answers and scores, reading status, bookmarks and notes are saved in your browser's local storage for this website. The website does not send this study information to us or to a server. It has no account login, advertising or added analytics scripts.

Progress is specific to this browser and website address. It does not automatically sync to another browser, device, domain or the Android app. Private browsing, clearing site data or browser storage limits may remove it. Export a backup if you want to keep a separate copy.

Hosting and content requests

Opening this website or refreshing its lesson catalogue sends normal web requests to the hosting service. When this edition is hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not they are signed into GitHub. GitHub handles hosting information under its GitHub General Privacy Statement. See also GitHub Pages data collection.

Content requests do not include your quiz answers, notes or saved progress. Internet access is needed to open or reload the website and retrieve updated lessons. This browser edition does not guarantee offline reopening.

Backups and deletion

Export downloads a JSON file containing your study progress and notes. Import reads a file you choose in your browser; the website processes it locally without uploading it. You control the file, its storage location and any sharing. Browser or device backup services may have their own policies.

Use the website's reset option to clear its saved progress, or clear this website's data in your browser settings. Separately downloaded backups remain until you delete them. Export before clearing data or switching website addresses if you need to retain your records.

External links

Official APPSC sources and other resource links open external websites. Their own privacy policies apply. This website does not request access to your location, contacts, camera or microphone.

Children

This website is designed for adults preparing for public-service recruitment examinations. It is not directed to children under 13.

Support

If you email support, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to respond. Avoid sending passwords, OTPR identifiers, identity documents or payment records. Contact nalamalan@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests to delete support correspondence, subject to necessary retention obligations.

Changes

Updates to this notice will appear here with a revised effective date. Hosting or data-handling changes require this notice to be reviewed.
